# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creative teams working with video, source files, contracts, and client delivery. They use Snip throughout active production, often moving large batches between projects and folders while continuing other review and delivery work.

## Product Purpose

Snip combines project files, video review, contracts, and paywalled delivery in one dependable workspace. Success means the tool feels like a normal project folder: organization is predictable, transfers are transparent and recoverable, and users can keep working without wondering whether a file is safe.

## Brand Personality

Simple, fast, reliable. Direct and tactile rather than ornamental; confident without becoming corporate or precious.

## Anti-references

Do not reproduce the slow, regressive, or opaque parts of Frame.io-style workflows. Avoid generic SaaS decoration, hidden background work, ambiguous drop targets, blocking batch operations, and feedback that arrives too late to build trust.

## Design Principles

1. Make file operations feel native and predictable.
2. Show work immediately, then keep progress and recovery visible.
3. Let users control one item without disrupting the rest of a batch.
4. Preserve context across navigation, reconnects, and relaunches.
5. Keep creative work primary; operational UI should be dense, calm, and easy to dismiss.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for authenticated product surfaces. All transfer controls and folder targets need keyboard-visible focus, descriptive labels, non-color status cues, 40px minimum hit areas, reduced-motion support, and live-region announcements that do not overwhelm assistive technology during frequent progress updates.
